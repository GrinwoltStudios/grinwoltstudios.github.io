About the game

Build your factory

Mine ore, smelt it in furnaces and turn it into parts in workshops and assemblers. Draw belts with your finger.

Balance the ratios

Every machine has a fixed speed: reach 100% efficiency, and lines that never stop become a Perfect Line.

12 eras, 5 maps

Advance from the Iron Age to the Space Age, open new maps and move materials with train stations.

Relaxing and deep

Pause in Build Mode to rearrange your layout. Your factory keeps working while you are away.
